    Support Buddy

    Is it really anonymous?

    Your username is never shown to the person you send to, and the app never shows anyone's location, phone or device. For private messages the system keeps a hidden code so a receiver can block someone who is being abusive. The receiver cannot see that code.

    Can I delete a message?

    No. Once it is posted it cannot be removed, so check it before you send.

    Why only 30 seconds of the song?

    The player uses the free 30-second preview. Tap the arrow to open the full song in Spotify.

    How do I get my messages?

    Claim a username to create a personal link. Messages sent through that link appear in your Inbox. Public Browse posts are not delivered to anyone’s Inbox.
